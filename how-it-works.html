  • 📊

2 📋 Made for your family

Build your personal plan

We prepare one complete plan that covers investing, protection and tax saving, and walk you through it in simple words. You decide what to go ahead with.

  • 📈

    Goal-wise investments

    The right SIPs and lump-sum investments for each goal, based on how far away it is.

3 ✍️ We handle the setup

Once you're happy with the plan, we take care of the paperwork so getting started is easy, and your family is protected from day one.

  • 🪪

    KYC and paperwork

    We guide you through KYC, forms and SIP mandates step by step.

  • 🔐

    Money stays in your name

    Your money goes directly to the fund house or insurer, in your own name. A2P never holds your money.

4 🔄 Every 3–6 months

Your plan isn't a one-time document. Every 3–6 months we review your portfolio with you, so it stays on track for good returns and your goals.

  • 📉

    Portfolio check-up

    How each investment is performing against your goals, explained clearly.

  • ⚖️

    Rebalance when needed

    We adjust your mix when markets move or a goal comes closer.

  • 👨‍👩‍👧

    Life changes, plan updates

    New job, new baby, new home or a bonus? We update your plan whenever life changes.

You get: peace of mind, and a team that's always a call away.

Questions

Common questions about the process

How long is the first meeting?

About 60 minutes. That's enough time to understand your goals, income and current investments properly, without rushing.

Do I have to invest immediately after the meeting?

No, there's no pressure to invest right away. Take your time to understand the plan, discuss it with your family and ask us any questions. You decide what to go ahead with, and when you're ready, we'll help you get started.

I already have some investments and policies. Can you still help?

Yes. We review what you already have and build your plan around it, so nothing is duplicated and any gaps are filled.

What happens during a review?

Every 3–6 months we go through how your investments are doing against your goals, rebalance if needed, and update the plan if anything in your life has changed.

Can I contact you between reviews?

Of course. Whether it's a new goal, a bonus to invest or a question about the market, our team is always just a call away.
